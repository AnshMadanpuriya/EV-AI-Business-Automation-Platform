Owner-manual range guidance:
- Typical city range under specified standard conditions:
  approximately 450 to 500 km
- Typical highway range under specified standard conditions:
  approximately 350 to 400 km
- Extended high-speed driving above 120 km/h:
  range may be below 300 km

Range guidance conditions:
- Driver plus one passenger
- Default or Range driving mode
- Regeneration level 1 or 2
- AC set to approximately 23–24 degrees Celsius
- Figures are guidance, not guaranteed certified range values.

Factors affecting range:
- Four or more passengers can reduce range by approximately 10–15%.
- Temperatures above 35 degrees Celsius can reduce city range
  by up to 25% and highway range by up to 15%.
- Temperatures below 15 degrees Celsius can reduce range
  by up to 15%.
- Uphill terrain may reduce range by approximately 20–25%,
  depending on gradient and duration.
- High speed, rain, snow, aggressive acceleration,
  heavy vehicle load and AC usage reduce range.

Suspension:
- Independent front suspension with iLink technology
- Independent 5-link rear suspension
- Semi-active suspension available on supported variants

Brakes:
- Front and rear disc brakes
- ABS
- Electronic Stability Program
- Electric Parking Brake

Wheels:
- 19-inch wheels
- Optional 20-inch wheels on supported variants

Important features:
- Coast-to-coast triple display on supported variants
- Harman Kardon audio with Dolby Atmos on supported variants
- Infinity Roof
- Multi-colour ambient lighting
- Connected vehicle technology
- Regenerative braking
- One-pedal driving
- Remote cabin cooling and heating
- 360-degree camera on supported variants
- Advanced parking assistance on supported variants
