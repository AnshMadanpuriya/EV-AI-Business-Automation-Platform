MODEL: MG COMET EV
==================================================

Category:
- Compact urban electric car
- Four-seat city EV
- Rear-wheel drive

Battery:
- Capacity: 17.3 kWh
- Type: Lithium-ion battery with prismatic cells
- Protection rating: IP67

Certified range:
- ARAI-certified range: 230 km per full charge

Expected real-world range:
- Approximately 170–200 km
- Depends on traffic, AC usage, speed, weather and driving style
- This is not a guaranteed range

Charging:
- 7.4 kW AC charger: approximately 3.5 hours
- 3.3 kW AC charger: approximately 7 hours
- DC fast charging: Not supported
- AC charging availability depends on the selected variant

Battery warranty:
- 8 years or 120,000 km
- Whichever occurs first
- Subject to MG warranty conditions

Seating:
- Four seats
- Second-row seat has 50:50 split arrangement

Important features:
- Dual 10.25-inch displays
- 10.25-inch infotainment screen
- 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster
- Wireless Android Auto
- Wireless Apple CarPlay
- i-SMART connected-car features
- More than 100 voice commands on supported variants
- Three drive modes
- Three KERS regenerative-braking modes
- Rotary gear selector
- Smart key on supported variants

Safety:
- Dual front airbags
- ABS with EBD
- Electronic Stability Control on supported variants
- Hill-hold control on supported variants
- Rear parking camera and sensors
- Tyre Pressure Monitoring System
- ISOFIX child-seat mounts
- High-strength body structure

Best suited for:
- Daily city commuting
- Short urban trips
- Buyers requiring a small and easily parked EV

Not ideal for:
- Regular long-distance highway journeys
- Users requiring DC fast charging
- Users requiring five seats

MODEL: MG WINDSOR EV
==================================================

Category:
- Electric CUV
- Five-seat family electric vehicle

Standard battery version:
- Battery capacity: 38 kWh
- Battery type: Lithium-ion
- Battery protection rating: IP67
- Certified range: approximately 332 km ARAI
- Maximum power: 100 kW / 136 PS
- Maximum torque: 200 Nm

Windsor PRO versions:
- Battery capacity: 52.9 kWh
- Certified range: 449 km MIDC P1+P2
- Maximum power: 100 kW / 136 PS
- Maximum torque: 200 Nm

Range warning:
- Certified range is measured under controlled test conditions
- Real-world range depends on speed, traffic, climate, AC usage,
  driving mode, vehicle load and battery condition
- Never promise 332 km or 449 km as guaranteed real-world range

Charging:
- Standard and PRO versions support AC and DC charging
- 7.4 kW AC home charger for 38 kWh version:
  approximately 6.5 hours from 0–100 percent
- 3.3 kW portable charger for 38 kWh version:
  approximately 13.8 hours
- Windsor PRO 7.4 kW AC charging:
  approximately 9.5 hours from 10–100 percent
- Windsor PRO 60 kW DC charging:
  approximately 50 minutes from 20–80 percent
- Actual charging time depends on battery temperature,
  charger output and charging conditions

Drive modes:
- Eco+
- Eco
- Normal
- Sport

Dimensions and space:
- Wheelbase: 2,700 mm
- Boot space: approximately 604 litres
- Aero Lounge rear seats can recline up to 135 degrees

Important features:
- 15.6-inch GRANDVIEW touchscreen
- Infinity View glass roof on supported variants
- Ventilated front seats on supported variants
- 256-colour ambient lighting on supported variants
- 360-degree surround-view camera on supported variants
- i-SMART connected-car technology
- More than 80 connected features
- More than 100 AI-based voice commands
- Wireless Android Auto and Apple CarPlay
- Powered tailgate on supported PRO variants
- Vehicle-to-Load on supported PRO variants
- Vehicle-to-Vehicle charging support on supported PRO variants
- Infinity audio system with nine speakers on Exclusive PRO
- 18-inch alloy wheels on supported variants

ADAS and safety:
- Level 2 ADAS on supported PRO variants
- ADAS features can include:
  - Adaptive Cruise Control
  - Traffic Jam Assist
  - Lane Departure Warning
  - Lane Departure Prevention
  - Lane Keep Assist
  - Forward Collision Warning
  - Automatic Emergency Braking
  - Intelligent Headlamp Control
- Six airbags on supported variants
- Electronic Stability Program
- Electronic parking brake
- Auto hold
- 360-degree camera on supported variants

BaaS explanation:
- BaaS means Battery-as-a-Service
- Under BaaS, the vehicle may have a lower initial purchase price
- The customer separately pays battery usage charges per kilometre
- BaaS is not free battery ownership
- Current prices and per-kilometre charges must be verified from MG

Best suited for:
- Families
- City and intercity travel
- Buyers wanting spacious rear seats and a large display

CHATBOT ACCURACY RULES
==================================================

1. Always distinguish certified range from real-world range.
2. Never guarantee the certified range.
3. Mention battery size along with the range.
4. Clarify whether the customer wants city, family, SUV,
   performance or luxury usage.
5. Do not recommend Comet EV when DC fast charging is mandatory.
6. Do not describe Cyberster as a family car.
7. Do not confuse Windsor 38 kWh with Windsor PRO 52.9 kWh.
8. ADAS and premium features may be variant-specific.
9. Charging time depends on charger power and battery conditions.
10. Prices and BaaS charges change frequently.
11. For current prices, variants and offers, direct the user to
    the official MG website or an authorised MG dealership.
12. Do not invent specifications that are absent from this document.
