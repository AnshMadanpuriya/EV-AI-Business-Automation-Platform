==================================================
RANGE AND CHARGING EXPLANATION
==================================================

MIDC:
- Modified Indian Driving Cycle
- Used for certified Indian range testing
- BMW iX1 LWB range is stated using MIDC

WLTP:
- Worldwide Harmonised Light Vehicle Test Procedure
- BMW iX, i5 and i7 ranges are commonly stated using WLTP
- MIDC and WLTP figures must not be treated as directly identical

Factors reducing real-world range:
- High speed
- Aggressive acceleration
- Low or high outside temperature
- Cabin heating or air conditioning
- Large wheels
- Vehicle load
- Road gradient
- Traffic
- Tyre pressure
- Battery age

Charging guidance:
- DC charging becomes slower after approximately 80 percent
- For routine use, BMW recommends a suitable charging target
- Repeatedly keeping a battery at 100 percent for long periods
  may not be ideal
- AC charging is generally suitable for regular overnight charging
- DC fast charging is useful during longer journeys

CHATBOT ACCURACY RULES
==================================================

1. Ask the user for the exact BMW model and variant.
2. Never confuse iX1 with iX.
3. Never confuse i5 with i7.
4. Never mix eDrive, xDrive and M variant specifications.
5. Explain whether the range figure is MIDC or WLTP.
6. Never guarantee certified range as real-world range.
7. Do not compare MIDC and WLTP without explaining the difference.
8. Do not assume every feature is standard on every variant.
9. Charging time depends on charger, temperature and battery level.
10. Prices change and should be checked on BMW India’s website.
11. Exact India-market equipment should be confirmed with BMW.
12. Never invent an unavailable battery or charging specification.
